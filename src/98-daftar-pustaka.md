# Daftar Pustaka

## Acuan utama

1. Hutton, G. *Programming in Haskell*, 2nd ed. Cambridge University Press, 2016.
2. Thompson, S. *Haskell: The Craft of Functional Programming*, 3rd ed. Addison-Wesley, 2011.
3. Scholl, P.C., Fauvet, M.C., Lagnier, F., Maraninchi, F. *Cours d'informatique: langage et programmation*. Masson, Paris, 1993. — *sumber asal notasi fungsional yang dipakai dalam diktat ini.*
4. Bird, R. *Thinking Functionally with Haskell*. Cambridge University Press, 2014.

## Bacaan pendukung

5. Hughes, J. "Why Functional Programming Matters". *The Computer Journal*, 32(2), 1989. — *argumen klasik, singkat, dan masih relevan.*
6. Abelson, H., Sussman, G.J., Sussman, J. *Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs*, 2nd ed. MIT Press, 1996.
7. Normand, E. *Grokking Simplicity: Taming Complex Software with Functional Thinking*. Manning, 2021. — *penerapan gagasan fungsional di luar bahasa fungsional.*
8. Okasaki, C. *Purely Functional Data Structures*. Cambridge University Press, 1998. — *lanjutan, untuk pembaca yang mempertanyakan efisiensi struktur data fungsional.*
9. Friedman, D., Felleisen, M. *The Little Schemer*, 4th ed. MIT Press, 1996.
10. Lipovača, M. *Learn You a Haskell for Great Good!* No Starch Press, 2011.
